VIRTUE STONE CASE — STAGE E CONTEXTUAL CHATTER REVIEW
PENDING HUMAN EDITORIAL AND GAMEPLAY ACCEPTANCE — November 4, 2026

Human gameplay testing demonstrated that the previously accepted remarks were too
long for normal overhead speech. The owner authorized shorter lines and more
contextual timing. This reopens only these four Stage E remarks and their timing;
Stage D Xanthia/William dialogue remains accepted and unchanged.

A — Iolo (preferred): These stones are filling our packs!
A — Shamino (fallback): Our packs are filling with stones.
B — Shamino (preferred): Knowest thou where each stone leads?
B — Dupre (fallback): Which stone leads where? I hope one leads to an inn!

A eligibility: at least three distinct original Virtue Stone colors held by the
party, including nested containers. Once observed, eligibility remains pending.
A context: actual addition of an individual real stone to party inventory,
including a native pickup or drag between inventory locations, or successful MARK.

B eligibility: two distinct party-held real stones with valid saved MARK
destinations. This approved approximation includes existing saves; it does not
reconstruct all historical casts. No per-stone history metadata is introduced.
B context: successful MARK, or a later native pickup/inventory move of a marked
stone. Unmarked stones and moving a container alone do not provide B context.
A context likewise requires the individual stone, not moving its whole container.

Opportunities last 15 seconds of normal engine ticks, permit brief deferral, and
are discarded on load. Close gumps promptly and stand quietly near the speaker.
Combat, conversations, protected sequences, gumps and other existing safety gates
still defer speech. Expiration retains eligibility for the next relevant action.
One delivery consumes both current opportunities: a single action cannot queue two
remarks. The second requires another relevant action and the existing minimum
five game minutes since the first. Recasting spells is never required: moving a
marked stone suffices. Loading, recruitment, unrelated inventory moves, failed MARK
and elapsed cooldown alone do not supply context.

Pending/completed bits and cooldown retain the existing VSCH version 1 format.
Older saves acquire eligibility on observation but need a fresh relevant action.
Completed hints never repeat. Actual completed-case delivery permanently suppresses
pending hints; commission/construction alone does not. No stone destinations,
spell requirements, original conversations or accepted renderer rules change.

Original companion dialogue was inspected for voice: Iolo's friendly observations,
Shamino's practical phrasing and Dupre's established inn/ale humor support these
exact owner candidates. All four stay ordinary incidental overhead speech.
Automation checks placement and state; reading comfort, voice, contextual feel and
unobtrusiveness require the owner's human review. No automatic editorial approval.
