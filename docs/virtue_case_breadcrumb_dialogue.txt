These exact four candidates are unchanged. Stage D remains accepted and unchanged.
Human testing first authorized shorter lines for transient overhead speech. The
owner then corrected the event-only design: companions must notice circumstances
organically, without requiring inventory shuffling or repeated spellcasting.

A: at least three distinct original Virtue Stone colors carried loose by the active
party, including ordinary nested containers. Real stones inside a case are excluded.
This retains the established three-color threshold; all eight are never required.
B: at least ONE distinct real party-held stone with a valid saved MARK destination,
as explicitly required by the new specification. Labels do not establish marking.
The prior two-marked-stone approximation is superseded by this one-stone minimum.
No historical per-stone metadata. Original stone identity/destination checks remain.

Natural delivery: existing ordinary game-minute updates revalidate current inventory
and quest state. Eligibility is sufficient; no new stone action is needed. A short
startup delay of at least ten engine seconds follows load or newly qualifying
circumstances. Then the next safe minute transition may deliver one observation.
Normal walking and quiet pauses qualify. No random chance can defer it indefinitely.
A remains preferred when both observations can speak. Five game minutes AND ten
engine seconds separate subsequent lines. Blocked states may extend these delays.

No 15-second event deadline remains. Successful MARK updates eligibility through
its existing hook, but neither MARK nor inventory movement is required. Loading an
older save with organized/marked stones works without changing them. Current state
is revalidated before speaking: dropping all stones removes eligibility, rather than
preserving stale requests. A deferred observation remains possible while its actual
conditions remain true. No speech request queue and no new permanent flags.

Only actual active, nearby, living, capable party members speak. Existing safety
gates protect combat, conversations, gumps, spellcasting, sleep, Native Wait,
transitions, overhead text and other protected conditions. The Native Wait service
is unchanged; the breadcrumb module additionally permits ordinary walking after
checking the remaining party safety conditions. Shared renderer/duration unchanged.

Case suppression: any real case currently owned by the party (also nested or in old
saves) suppresses BOTH observations. Quest DELIVERED is authoritative and permanent,
even after dropping/losing the case. Commission/construction/ready alone do not
suppress. For a nonquest case, current ownership is derived from existing inventory;
dropping it can restore undelivered eligibility if no completed quest exists. No
second acquisition history or fabricated quest completion is introduced.

VSCH version 1 pending/completed bits and saved cooldown remain compatible. Completed
remarks never repeat through another companion or after save/load. Pending bits are
reconstructed/revalidated on load and periodic updates, not treated as historical
proof after the underlying conditions disappear. Startup pacing is transient.
