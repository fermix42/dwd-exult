Local unpublished changes after rc.2, November 5, 2026

Reported symptom: Trinsic pub patrons' barks remained visible in the swamp.
The renderer clamped every overhead message into the viewport, even from distant
world anchors. The first local fix hid every fully off-screen world speaker.
The owner then identified the regression: Finnigan's opening summons is a valid
off-screen call. That strict screen-boundary rule is superseded below.

Current policy:
- On-screen speakers keep the accepted placement, wrapping and duration.
- Nearby actor speech remains visible within a ten-tile margin around the game
  view, matching existing NPC proximity processing and world scaling.
- Actors approaching under talk or arrest_avatar schedules retain directed calls
  within the existing 50-tile approach range, on the current map. There is no actor
  ID, dialogue-string or mod override.
- Other world text outside its applicable visibility region has no visible layer.
- Gump placement and captured lifetimes are preserved. Hidden speech expires normally.

fresh disposable profiles and real read-only game/mod assets. Camera fixtures do
not move actors or force quests, schedules or story flags.
