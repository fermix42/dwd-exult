TOPIC: Virtue Stone case — XANTHIA

"I should like to commission a carrying case for the Virtue Stones," you say.

"Virtue Stones? I am not familiar with them. Might I see one?"

You show Xanthia one of the stones.

"Oh, how lovely! And quite unusual. Tell me, what sort of case hast thou in mind?"

"Something sturdy, yet small enough to carry on my travels. I should hate to have such precious things rattling about in my pack," you reply.

"Hmm. A fine wooden case, lined with soft cloth to protect the stones. Aye, I believe I could fashion something suitable."

"Good timber, and soft cloth for the lining. We must give those stones a proper home! The Guild's work and fittings will cost one hundred gold."

TOPIC: commission case — XANTHIA

"I shall be pleased to make it. Ask William to send the timber here, and bring me the cloth and our fee. Once we have everything, we shall need two days for the work."

TOPIC: timber delivery — WILLIAM

"I require some fine timber. Couldst thou arrange its delivery to the Artist's Guild?" you ask.

"Aye, I have suitable planks. Twenty gold for the wood and delivery to the Guild. Dost thou wish to buy them?"

TOPIC: buy timber — WILLIAM

"Thou hast not the twenty gold. Come back when thou canst pay, and I shall arrange the delivery."

"Very well. I shall have the planks sent to the Artist's Guild."

TOPIC: give cloth — XANTHIA

"Have we forgotten the lining? Bring me a length of cloth. I would not have those lovely stones knocking against bare wood."

"Yes, this will make a fine lining! I shall put it aside for thy case."

TOPIC: pay Guild fee — XANTHIA

"Not quite enough, I fear. The Guild must have one hundred gold for the work and fittings. Bring it when thou art able."

"Splendid! That takes care of the Guild's fee."

TOPIC: collect case — XANTHIA

"Thy case is ready, but there is no room for it on the table. Clear a space and I shall set it out."

"Thy case is finished, Avatar! Come, I shall set it upon the table for thee."

TOPIC: case progress — XANTHIA

"Patience, Avatar! I would not have thee carrying a poorly made case because we hurried the work. Give us a little more time."

WHEN CLOTH OR PAYMENT COMPLETES ALL REQUIREMENTS — XANTHIA

"There, that is everything! We can get to work on thy case now."
